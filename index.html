Platform Mission & Purpose

Building a Resilient Organic Supply Chain for Indonesia

Organic residues represent over 40% of Indonesia's municipal waste stream. Rather than filling open landfills and releasing harmful methane, SIRKULA redirects pre-sorted organic streams directly into regenerative local uses.

Methane Prevention at Source

By collecting clean kitchen trimmings and market surplus before anaerobic rotting begins in municipal dumps, organic nutrients are safeguarded and greenhouse gas emissions are directly averted.

Source-Segregated Traceability

Decentralized Local Exchanges

Long transportation routes increase spoilage and transport costs. SIRKULA connects food enterprises with nearby micro-cultivators, urban compost facilities, and peri-urban smallholder farms.

Low-Radius Logistical Pairing

National Circular Alignment

Structured in harmony with Indonesia's Circular Economy Roadmap, transforming waste management from an expensive disposal cost into a regenerative feedstock for protein and soil restoration.

How SIRKULA Works

A frictionless pathway enabling hospitality and culinary businesses to redirect food residues safely into the hands of responsible upcyclers.

1
Food Businesses

Report Organic Waste

Restaurants, cafes, hotels, and food markets log their sorted organic surplus, specifying volume, feedstock type, and pickup window.

2
Collectors

Connect & Request

Composters, BSF insect breeders, and livestock owners search listings within their radius, inspect purity ratings, and request matching batches.

3
Circular Ecosystem

Collect & Reuse

Waste is collected during the designated handover window and directly converted into high-grade compost, protein-dense insect feed, or animal nourishment.

Upcycling Pathways

Waste Utilization Categories

Not all organic waste is equal. We categorize feedstock to ensure every organic fraction meets its optimal, biologically safe valorization route.

Soil Enrichment

1. Composting

Vegetable peelings, spent coffee grounds, dry leaves, and garden off-cuts are transformed via microbial decomposition into humus-rich organic soil amendment.

Protein Bioconversion

2. BSF Cultivation

Black Soldier Fly larvae rapidly consume diverse food scraps, yielding high-protein larval meal for aquaculture and poultry, while leaving behind frass biofertilizer.

Livestock Nutrition

3. Animal Feed

Clean, unadulterated bakery surpluses, spent brewer and tofu grains, and fresh whole produce are safely redirected to nourish local farm animals.

About Our Vision

Pioneering Indonesia's Closed-Loop Food Sustainability

SIRKULA — Sistem Integrasi Rantai Keberlanjutan Limbah Organik Indonesia — was conceptualized to address one of the archipelago's most critical environmental challenges: the sheer volume of organic nutrients lost to unmanaged municipal landfills.

Instead of viewing organic by-products as waste requiring costly disposal, our infrastructure treats each fraction as an essential industrial and agricultural resource. We bridge the logistical gap between urban food service businesses and regional agricultural innovators.

"Every kilogram of organic matter kept out of a landfill is clean soil restored and methane prevented."
